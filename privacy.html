How shared details are handled

What you share

Only what you choose. If you type your details, we send exactly what you entered. If you share from your own VALE card, you tick each detail to include, and we read those details from your saved card. Nothing is sent until you tick the consent box and choose Share.

Who receives it

Only the owner of the card you shared with. Your details are added to their private connections, which only they can see. VALE Connect never tells you whether they already had your details.

What we keep

We keep a record of each share: what you shared, when, and the version of the consent wording you agreed to. A share that became one of the owner’s connections is kept for as long as that connection exists. Other share records are removed after 90 to 180 days. Notifications to the owner are removed after 90 days once read, or after a year if unread.

Protection against misuse

A security check from Cloudflare (Turnstile) confirms that a person is sharing. To limit repeated or automated sharing, we count recent shares from each network and from each email address or phone number. For this count we keep only scrambled codes, made with a secret key from your network address and from the email address or phone number you shared. The codes can’t be turned back into the address or number, and we never store your network address itself. The codes are kept for 48 hours, then removed the next time sharing runs.

This count is separate from the card owner’s copy: the details you chose to share stay readable in their private connections, so they can contact you.

Changing your mind

Ask the card’s owner to delete your details from their connections. Card owners can also turn off sharing on their card at any time.
