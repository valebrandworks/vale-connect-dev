Privacy Policy

Terms of Service · Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

VALE Connect is a digital business card and contact app run by VALE Brandworks LLC, an Indiana limited liability company ("VALE", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and your choices. It applies to the VALE Connect app, public card pages and the emails we send.

In short: we use your information to run VALE Connect and for nothing else. We don't sell it and we don't show ads. The VALE Connect app contains no advertising or analytics code and does not use advertising cookies. VALE Connect is offered to people aged 18 and over in the United States.

1. Information we collect

Your account. Your email address and password (our sign-in provider stores the password only in scrambled, hashed form; we can never see it); when you created your account and last signed in; the version and time of the Privacy Policy and Terms you agreed to, and your confirmation that you are 18 or older and live in the United States (we record what you confirmed; we don't check your age or location); your plan (for example Free) and usage counts such as how many cards you have.

Your digital cards. What you choose to add: name, title, company, a short introduction, email, phone, website, social and other links, a photo, a business logo, an appearance style and the card's link name; also an optional private card name only you see. A card is a private draft (only you can see it) or public (see section 3).

Your connections. Details you save about people you meet: name, company, title, email, phone, website, links, your private notes, categories, follow-up dates, next steps, where and how you met, who introduced you, and dated notes. Connections are private to your account.

When someone shares their details with you. On a public card, visitors and members can send you their contact details ("Share your details back"). We record the details they chose to send and any note; which card they shared with, when, and the version of the consent wording they agreed to; and, for members, which of their own cards the details came from.

When someone reports a card. Anyone can report a public card, without an account. We record the card and a copy of its public details at that moment, the reason chosen and any explanation, an email address only if the reporter chooses to give one, and a scrambled code from the reporter's network address.

Security and technical information.

  • Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a person, not an automated program, is signing in, creating an account, resetting a password, sharing details, reporting a card or confirming account deletion. It looks at technical signals from your browser and device.
  • Scrambled codes. To limit repeated or automated sharing and reporting, we briefly keep scrambled codes made with a secret key from the sender's network address and, for sharing, from the email or phone number shared. They cannot be turned back into the address or number, and we never store the network address itself.
  • Sign-up screening. We refuse sign-ups from certain temporary email services. When we refuse one, we record only the provider's domain name (never the address).
  • Statistics. Once a day we store counts only (new accounts, confirmed accounts, emails sent, refused sign-ups, shares and reports accepted or refused), with no personal details.
  • Provider logs. Our hosting and service providers keep technical logs (network address, browser type, time and type of request) to run, secure and troubleshoot their services (section 4).

On your device. VALE Connect does not set advertising or tracking cookies and has no advertising or analytics services. It uses your browser's storage only to keep you signed in; to keep any card or connections saved only in that browser, from before you used an account, until you import or remove them; to remember that you already shared your details with a card (the date only); and to store app files for offline use (never your account data or contacts). Our security-check provider (Cloudflare Turnstile) runs in its own frame, and it and our hosting and delivery providers may use cookies or similar technologies for their own security and operation; we don't control them, and they are governed by their own policies.

2. How we use information

  • Running the service: creating and running your account, cards and connections, and showing public cards to people you share them with.
  • Sharing: delivering details people choose to share with you and showing them to you in the app.
  • Account emails: confirmation, password-reset and other essential account and service emails (security notices such as "password changed"). We don't send marketing emails; any optional updates in future would need your separate consent and an unsubscribe link in each message.
  • Security and abuse prevention: security checks, rate limits, spam and fraud prevention, and reviewing reports about public cards.
  • Support and legal duties: answering questions, enforcing the Terms and meeting legal obligations.

We don't sell your personal information, share it for targeted advertising or use it for profiling. We don't track you across websites, so we don't change how we operate in response to Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signals.

3. Public cards, and what others can see

  • Public cards: anyone with your card's link or QR code can see its details, including your photo and logo, and can save them to a phone or to their own VALE Connect account. Publish only what you are comfortable sharing publicly. Making a card private again stops new visitors, but we can't remove copies people already saved.
  • Private information: private drafts, private card names, connections, notes and account details are never shown to other users.
  • Sharing back: if you share your details through someone's card, they receive exactly what you chose. VALE Connect never tells you whether they already had your details. Card owners can turn this feature off for any card.
  • Administrators: a small number of VALE administrators can review reports about public cards, after signing in with two-step verification. They never see reporters' network addresses.

How cleanup runs. Report records, their scrambled codes, and the sign-up screening log and statistics are cleaned automatically every day. Sharing records (the scrambled codes, duplicate and over-limit shares, and notifications) are cleaned automatically whenever sharing is used or you open your notifications, so these records can remain longer than the periods shown. If nothing triggers that cleanup, a record can stay for a long time after its period ends. For these records the periods are targets for removal, not a promise that a record disappears when its period ends. We may keep information longer where the law requires, or to resolve disputes or security incidents.

6. Deleting your account

We permanently delete your account, cards, photos and logos, connections and notes, the shares you received, your notifications and plan record. Your public card links stop working and their link names are retired. Copies in backups are overwritten within about 7 days. If someone reported one of your cards, our record of that report and our decision (including a copy of the card's public details at that time) is kept for the period in section 5 even after you delete your account. Details you shared with other people become part of their own records, so deleting your account doesn't remove them; ask those people directly. Anything saved only in a browser stays there until you remove it or clear that browser's data.

7. Security

We protect information with encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, database rules that let each account reach only its own data, private file storage, security checks, rate limits and two-step sign-in for administrators. No online service can guarantee perfect security; please use a strong, unique password. If a breach affects your personal information, we'll notify you as the law requires.

8. Your rights

You can see, correct, export and delete your information yourself in the app. You can also email privacy@valebrandworks.com to ask us to confirm whether we process your information and give you a copy, correct or delete it, or give it to you in a portable format. We offer these to everyone, whatever state you live in. Some state privacy laws, including Indiana's Consumer Data Protection Act, may also give you these rights, and a right to appeal our decision; to appeal, reply to our decision. We may need to verify your identity first, and we won't treat you differently for using your rights.

9. Age requirement

VALE Connect is only for people aged 18 or older. Sign-up asks you to confirm that you are 18 or older; we don't verify your age. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Where information is stored and processed

VALE Connect is offered in the United States. Your account, card, connection and sharing data is stored in our database in the United States (US East). Some of our providers (Cloudflare, Resend and jsDelivr) run global networks, so technical information about a request or an email (such as your network address, browser details and email delivery data) may be handled in other countries as it is routed. We don't choose that routing.

11. Changes to this policy

We'll post changes here and update the effective date. For material changes we'll tell you by email and in the app, ordinarily at least 14 days before they take effect. If you keep using VALE Connect after a change takes effect, you accept it; if you don't agree, you can delete your account before then. Where the law requires your express consent, we'll ask for it.
